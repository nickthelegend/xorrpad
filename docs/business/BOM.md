# Production BOM and purchasing basis

Research date: **2026-11-12**. USD, before tax, freight, assembler procurement fees, attrition, reels and minimum-order rounding. [Pro CSV](bom-pro.csv) and [Core CSV](bom-core.csv) contain separate rows at **100 / 500 / 1,000 / 5,000 finished pads**. These are design choices requested in the [brief](BRIEF.md). They reconcile board references to the [SKiDL manifests](../../hardware/generated/) and add final-assembly kits. Every priced row has its URL, read date, selected published quantity tier and stock snapshot. Blank means **quote needed**, never zero.

**Derived reference subtotals, not purchasable BOM totals or landed costs.** Generated by [business-bom.py](../../tools/business-bom.py) from [saved distributor facts](price-sources.json). The Pro subtotal falls sharply because the display vendor explicitly requests quotes at 500+; its price is then blank and excluded. That is missing coverage, not a manufacturing saving. Stock shortages are flagged even when a reference price exists. A quoted replacement microphone, full power circuit, caps, case and cable will change these numbers.

For an order of N pads, required component quantity is `N × quantity per pad`: sockets and switches are **14N**; Core LEDs are **15N** (14 keys plus status); Pro has one status LED. Use the largest published tier threshold not exceeding the required component quantity. Do not interpolate a new discount. A quantity beyond the last tier uses that published rate only as a reference. The CSV is not a cart: supplier package multiples, scrap/rework, panel rails and assembly attrition still need a purchasing quote. **ASSUMPTION:** reserve 2% extra pads/parts for pilot yield planning; this is not measured production yield or included in the table.

### Bare display versus breakout

EastRising **ER-TFT028-4** is the bare ILI9341 FPC candidate. Its public base product shows **$7.08 single, $6.91 at 10, $6.73 at 30, $6.54 at 50, $6.35 at 100; 500+ quote request**. Touch and connector options are configurable: quote needed from EastRising for the exact **no-touch** production variant and all higher runs; do not assume optional touch is free or invent a bulk price. [Product](https://www.buydisplay.com/serial-spi-2-8-tft-lcd-module-display-320x240-optional-touch-screen), read 2026-11-12 (indexed page accessible; direct fetch later returned HTTP 403).

Matching top-contact **ER-CON50HT-1**: **$0.29 single, $0.20 at 1,000**, reel 2,000 with cut tape offered. [Connector](https://www.buydisplay.com/50-pin-0-5mm-pitch-top-contact-zif-connector-fpc-connector), read 2026-11-12. Connector is a separate BOM line; assembler must accept consigned parts. Panel datasheet Rev2.1 says 50 pins, 0.5 mm pitch, four parallel backlight LEDs and 3.3 V maximum operating logic supply; this panel does **not** need an assumed series-LED boost. The draft uses individually limited anodes and a PWM cathode transistor. [Manufacturer PDF via distributor mirror](https://www.icbanq.com/icdownload/V2_DATA/ICBShop/Board/DFDSAFJLSDA.pdf), read 2026-11-12. FPC contact orientation, strain relief, current and the custom connector land pattern still need review.

Adafruit's comparable ILI9341 breakout is **$29.95 single, $26.96 at 10–99, $23.96 at 100+**; it includes touch, microSD, regulation and level shifting. This proves a breakout costs more for that configuration, not a universal display-cost rule or a like-for-like margin saving. [Adafruit 1770](https://www.adafruit.com/product/1770), read 2026-11-12.
