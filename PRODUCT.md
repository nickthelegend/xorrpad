# Product

## Platform
Web application in Electron, also served at phone widths.

## Users and purpose
XOR Desktop is the companion for xorrpad, a product of xorr.finance. Owners
connect their pad, program keys, install abilities, configure their own agent,
and inspect or accept gated proposals. Markets is a secondary workspace.

## Stack and constraints
The v2 brief explicitly selects Vite, React and Tailwind v4 in desk/app.
The existing authenticated backend and /events supply real state. Preserve v1
at /v1 and Classic at /classic. No invented prices, receipts or device status.
Phone signing is default; capped opt-in session signing retains its v1 gate.
Our transaction tests only send on devnet. Secrets belong in OS safeStorage.

## Brand commitments
Inherit xorr.finance's exact brand and supplied source components. The user's
v2 brief settles the device-first structure, six screens and command palette.
No alternate visual direction, pricing, customer or manufacturing claims are
introduced. Hardware remains compiled, not yet flashed.
