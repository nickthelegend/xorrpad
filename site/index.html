A bigger picture.

The keys are just the beginning. The desk connects your intent to quotes, checks and a wallet signature.

  1. 1 / PAD

    Make your move.

    Use a key, a voice command or the desk to create a trade proposal. Connect the pad over USB, Wi-Fi or BLE; physical radio testing is still ahead.

  2. 2 / DESK

    See the whole trade.

    Compare available routes, review fees and run the proposal through your permission gate. Bring your own model and provider keys.

  3. 3 / VENUES

    Choose. Then sign.

    Accept or refuse the proposal. Phone-wallet signing is the default; an optional funded session wallet operates within your caps.

Fast hands.
Deliberate decisions.

Every proposal passes through a gate before signing. Set per-trade and daily caps, leverage limits, venue and market allowlists, and a cooldown.

Manual acceptance is the default. Auto-accept is off until enabled under your policy, and each automatic acceptance is logged and spoken.

Trading can lose money. Permission limits reduce what the app may do; they do not eliminate market or execution risk.

A few things
to know.

Can I buy a finished device today?

No. This is a workbench prototype. The order of work is a working ESP32-S3 demo, then waitlist and reservations, then bulk manufacturing. There is no promised ship date.

Does the pad hold my wallet keys?

The pad sends intent to the desk. The default signing path is your phone wallet. The optional session wallet is held by Electron safeStorage on the desktop and must be explicitly created and funded. Never share a seed phrase through this site.

Can it trade automatically?

Auto-accept is off by default and constrained by your permission policy. Acceptance and signing are separate steps. Mainnet session signing needs the explicit real-funds acknowledgement in the desk.

Do I need an AI subscription?

v1 uses your own model and keys, such as a local Ollama model or a supported provider. Voice uses Deepgram credentials. A paid hosted model subscription is outside v1.

What are the reservation and refund terms?

They are not published yet. Nick must write and publish them at PREORDER_TERMS_URL. Payments stay disabled until the terms, price and merchant are configured. Read the published terms before reserving.
